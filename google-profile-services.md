## ⭐ Review Strategy

### How to Get More Reviews

1. **Send WhatsApp reminders** after order delivery with a direct Google review link.
2. **Print a QR Code** that links to your Google review page — place it at your counter.
3. **Ask happy customers** in person to leave a review.
4. **Follow up via SMS** with a short message and review link.

### Review Response Templates

**For 5-star reviews:**
```
Thank you so much, [Name]! We're thrilled you loved our work.
It was a pleasure serving you at Kashish Ads.
We look forward to helping you again! — Team Kashish Ads
```

**For negative reviews:**
```
Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We're sorry to hear about your experience.
Please contact us at [Phone/Email] so we can resolve this for you immediately.
Your satisfaction is our top priority. — Team Kashish Ads
```
